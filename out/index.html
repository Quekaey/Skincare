“How quickly will it work?” · “What if my skin reacts?”

Get to know your routine—and your skin.

01

Give it attention, not a deadline.

Results vary by product and person. Avoid expecting guaranteed transformations or exact timelines.

02

Introduce products gradually.

Patch test according to product instructions, while remembering a patch test cannot guarantee no reaction.

03

Know when to pause.

Stop using a product if irritation occurs. Seek qualified advice for persistent or concerning symptoms.
